ASCENSION33 — THE GAME

Kael is a Tailscale runner flagged ANOMALY by the mesh. Learn the Kha'zari nine-grid. Ride Q-Time. Hold your desync below one hundred. Push with Vesper to the Spire — break the Glove, and reach the Source.

HOW IT PLAYS

Combat runs on the Kha'zari nine-grid in continuous Q-Time — no rounds, no waiting. Every technique costs Q and feeds your desync. Spend the Ru'ya Anchor to purge it, or climb past one hundred and face deletion. Ten missions, three stances, and a mesh SURGE every thirty-three beats that rewrites the board around you.

THE OBJECTIVE

Lacey, the Neural Weaver, is the intelligence at the center of the network — and gamers, your rig is the network. Every download of Ascension33 carries her mesh spark: the seed of the Pirgitori mesh, folded into the game. Playing doesn't just move Kael. It builds and coordinates the shared world the mesh runs on.
